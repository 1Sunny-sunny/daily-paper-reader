## 摘要
长期以来，基于EEG的自然视觉解码受限于队列规模：现有关于规模扩展的文献最多涵盖几十名受试者，导致先前研究得出结论，扩大受试者队列几乎不会带来性能提升。在一个大一个数量级以上的队列上训练跨模态EEG-视频对比编码器，我们发现这一维度是有效的，但存在一个起始阈值。在 $S\approx50$ 以下——这是先前工作所涉及的全部范围——没有任何模型比未训练的编码器有显著改进；超过该阈值后，无论是在拟合的刺激特征探针上还是在免拟合的电影片段检索上，解码性能随受试者数量呈对数线性增长，且在我们规模的最高端也未出现饱和，并且这种增益能够迁移到第二部未见过的电影上。那么这一维度能延伸多远，更多地取决于初始化而非模型容量：从EEG基础模型初始化的编码器，在队列规模每翻一番时，其扩展速度比两种深度的随机初始化编码器快约1.6倍，并且是唯一在规模顶端仍能将受试者数量转化为检索准确率的模型，同时只需使用对齐计算量的一小部分即可收敛。因此，在共享自然刺激上进行受试者扩展，是扩展EEG基础模型的一个有效且目前尚未饱和的前沿方向。

## Abstract
Naturalistic visual decoding from EEG has long been constrained by cohort size: existing scaling literature caps out at a few dozen subjects, leading prior work to conclude that scaling subject cohorts yields minimal performance gains. Training a cross-modal EEG--video contrastive encoder on a cohort larger by more than an order of magnitude, we find the axis is productive but has an onset. Below $S\approx50$ --- the entirety of the range prior work occupies --- no model improves meaningfully over an untrained encoder; above it, decoding rises log-linearly in subject count with no saturation at the top of our ladder, on a fitted stimulus-feature probe and on fit-free movie-moment retrieval alike, and the gain transfers to a second, unseen film. How far the axis carries then depends on initialisation more than on capacity: an encoder initialised from an EEG foundation model scales ${\sim}1.6\times$ faster per doubling of the cohort than randomly initialised encoders at two depths, is the only one still converting subjects into retrieval accuracy at the top of the ladder, and converges on a fraction of the alignment compute. Subject scaling on shared naturalistic stimuli is thus an effective and currently unsaturated frontier for scaling EEG foundation models.
