## 摘要
脑磁图（MEG）是一种成像技术，能够以非侵入方式、毫秒级分辨率观察人脑活动。随着MEG数据日益丰富，我们有机会利用人工智能的最新进展——自监督基础模型。现有的MEG（及脑电图）基础模型均基于Transformer架构。本文介绍MEG-Mamba：一个基于Mamba架构的神经活动（源重建、分区后的MEG）生成式基础模型。MEG-Mamba经过训练，在给定脑区和记录会话的条件下预测离散化MEG信号的下一个token。与基于Transformer的替代模型（MEG-GPT）相比，MEG-Mamba在生成保真度上更优，同时预训练耗时更短（22对400 GPU小时），并能建模更长上下文（4秒对0.32秒）。我们通过考察其生成数据的时空频谱特征并解释其学习到的嵌入来评估MEG-Mamba。此外，我们证明轻量级刺激条件化（LoRA）可用于生成未包含在预训练中的真实任务诱发响应。结果表明，Mamba是扩展神经基础模型规模的有前景的主干架构。

## Abstract
Magnetoencephalography (MEG) is an imaging technique that offers a non-invasive, millisecond-resolution view of human brain activity. The increasing availability of MEG data presents an opportunity to take advantage of a recent advance in artificial intelligence, namely self-supervised foundation models. Existing foundation models for MEG (and electroencephalography) have been built on a transformer architecture. Here, we introduce MEG-Mamba: a generative foundation model for neural activity (source reconstructed, parcellated MEG) built on a Mamba architecture. MEG-Mamba is trained to predict the next token of a discretised MEG signal, conditioned on a brain region and recording session. MEG-Mamba surpasses the generative fidelity of a transformer-based alternative (MEG-GPT) while pre-training in less time (22 vs 400 GPU-hours) and modelling a longer context (4 vs 0.32 s). We evaluate MEG-Mamba by examining the spatio-spectral characteristics of the data it generates and by interpreting its learned embeddings. Furthermore, we demonstrate that lightweight stimulus conditioning (LoRA) can be used to generate realistic task-evoked responses that are not included in pre-training. Our results suggest that Mamba is a promising backbone for scaling neural foundation models.
